How Does Solar Energy Works?

Solar radiation is the electromagnetic radiation or also known as sunlight emitted by the sun. It can be turn into useful forms of energy using solar technologies.
There are two main types of solar technologies:

  • Photovoltaics (PV)
  • Concentrating solar-thermal power (CSP)


Photovoltaics (PV)

When the sun shines onto a solar panel, the energy from the sunlight is absorb by the PV cells in the panel. This energy creates electrical charges that move in response to an internal electrical field in the cell, causing electricity to flow. Then, the electricity is convert from direct-current (DC) to alternating current (AC) to power the appliances in the building.


An individual PV is very small and only produce about 1 to 2 watts of power. Therefore, they are connected together in chains to form larger units known as panels or modules. The number of the panels can be modified for different electric power need.


Concentrating Solar-Thermal Power (CSP)

CSP systems use mirrors to reflect and concentrate sunlight onto receivers that collect solar energy. After that, this energy is used to heat the fluid in the receiver to generate thermal energy that can be used to generate electricity. Because it requires a lot of space, it is primarily used in very large power plants.
