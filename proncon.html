Solar Energy

What is Solar Energy?

Solar energy is power that comes from sunlight. Solar panels catch sunlight and turn it into electricity that homes and businesses can use.

Pros

  • Renewable, the sun will not run out
  • Lowers your electricity bill
  • Low cost to maintain after install
  • No pollution while running
  • Works in remote areas with no power grid
  • Can raise property value
  • Getting cheaper every year

Cons

  • Expensive to install at first
  • Needs sunlight, weak on cloudy days
  • Needs a lot of space
  • Batteries to store power are costly
  • Making panels creates some waste
  • Not every building can fit panels

Bottom Line

Solar energy is clean and saves money in the long run. The biggest problem is the starting cost, but it usually pays itself back over time. If you have sunlight and some budget, solar is a smart choice.
