Application Of Solar Energy

Residential solar panel

By installing solar panels on the rooftops

homeowners can use energy of the sun to generate electricity

Can reduce monthly utility bills

Transportation

Solar panels installed on the roofs or other parts of vehicle capture sunlight and convert it into electricity and used to charge the vehicle’s battery

extend the driving ranges and capabilities of vehicles

Solar Power Plant

Large-scale solar system that convert solar energy to electrical energy

It usually used by private companies to supply electricity to their own building

It offers the most cost-effective method of generating electricity

Powering small devices

Install the portable solar panels on small devices

Offer a convenient and sustainable powering solution for small devices

Avoid disposable batteries

Public infrastructure

Provide energy for street lights, traffic signals and public charging stations for electric vehicle

Improve energy efficiency and reduce electricity costs
