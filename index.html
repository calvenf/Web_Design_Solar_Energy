What is Solar Energy?

Solar energy in simple terms is the energy soource, harnessed by humans from the sun, to power all sorts of electronics and other electrical related equipment. Although solar energy has been in mass market production and usage across industries and also in residential enviroment, it however has only recently gained advancement in its Research and Development.

Why Use Solar Energy?

Solar energy is the future of clean energy. It is one of the most promising and least polluting of other clean energy sources. Solar energy is also an energy source that is already in full industry implementation and a more increasingly popular choice of energy because of its sustainability.

Solar energy is becoming popular not only to businesses looking to save a dime on the dollar in terms of energy usage but also for the younger generation who are faced with expesnive electriticy bills. Solar panels are a great investment for people of all ages.

Solar Panels

Solar panels are currently the most used and common devices used to harness the sun's solar radiation and convert it into usable electricity. Although currently only accessbible to certain groups of people in society, it will however will eventually become more accessible to the general public. Goverments will also soon start offering subsidies in order to promote higher usage of solar panels for everyone.

United Nations Sustainable Development Goals

In line with the United Nations Sustaianbility Development Goals or UNSDGs, renewable energy has become a huge part of the goals that are set to be achieved by 2030. Although it seems unlikely for now, in the next 5 years,an increase in the usage and implementations of solar energy will be seen globaally. The goal related to solar energy and renewable energy as a whole is Goal 7: Affordable and Clean Energy. This goal aims to promote the use of renewable energy, especially in developing countries. .

UNSDG Goal 7: Affordable and Clean Energy

As previously mentioned, UNSDG Goal 7 aims to promote the use of renewable energy, especially in developing countries. In recent years since the annoucnement and implemnetation of the SDG goals, the world has seen an increase in terms of global access to electricity, jumping from 84% into 2010 to a respectable 92% by 2023. This increase of almost 8% is already impressive, proving that the goals work and that we can certainly acheive electricity for the whole world regardless of economic status. While it is impressive that the world has an increase in terms of global access to electricity, we as society still have long way to acheive the goal of affordable and clean energy for all of society.

Important Targets Related to UNSDG Goal 7

There are 5 key targets that the United Nations has set in order to achieve UNSDG Goal 7:

Target 7.1

To ensure that by 2030, all people have access to affordable, reliable and modern energy services.

Target 7.2

To increase substantially the share of renewable energy in the global energy mix.

Target 7.3

To double the rate of improvement in energy efficiency by 2030.

Target 7.a

Enhance international cooperation to improve access to clean energy research, technology, and investment in sustainable energy infrastructure.

Target 7.b

Expand infrastructure and upgrade technology to provide modern, sustainable energy services in developing countries, especially least developed, small island, and landlocked nations.
